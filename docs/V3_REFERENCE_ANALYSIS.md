## What makes the reference engaging

The central finding is artifact continuity: a project is a technical sheet, a film has a screening space, a photograph belongs to a collection, and interests have tactile objects. Each presentation fits its content. A stable navigation frame lets those spaces differ without becoming unrelated websites. This principle is more useful than copying a particular animation.

| Reference concept (public-source observation) | Why it works / principle | Original application and improvement for this portfolio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hash-routed sections retain an active indicator, titles and focus handling. | A recognizable frame gives visitors orientation across different content. | Real Next.js routes inside an original source-list workspace. Keep SSR, direct URLs, refresh and history; no gated single-page entrance. |

Prefer immediate reading and small continuity cues to another introductory performance. |

Reading is primary; no invented notebook observations or results. |

Keep the original complete write-ups available. |
| Project drawings react to intentional interaction. | Motion rewards inquiry into the subject rather than decorating every surface. | Method pathways and optional accurate scientific details associated with actual projects. Never present conceptual artwork as experimental data. |
| Several document types share one reader with zoom, fit, download and original-file access. | A familiar tool reduces the effort of exploring different artifacts. | Retain the working pinned local PDF/media viewer as a shared V3 utility. Improve focus recovery, mobile sizing and failed-resource access rather than cloning the reference's window chrome. |

## Deliberate departures

Do not reproduce the reference's scroll-locked mosaic entrance, timed biography typing, branded console/shop tribute, automatic card rotation, hash-only architecture, or remotely hosted document library. Those choices are distinctive to that owner or create avoidable friction here. The portfolio should be original, calm at rest, and fully usable before optional enhancement.
