## 3. One definitive visual system
Combine Living Matter's motion with Field Atlas's archival precision. Warm parchment #F1EBE0, paper #FFFDF9, ink #292720, accessible secondary #625D53, deep sage #426662, sage #719D9A, rule #D4CCBD, cinema #10191B, energy #54C8F4, amber #CFAB7D. Blue glow is a rare cinematic accent, not a persistent decoration. Keep EB Garamond, Instrument Sans, Courier Prime local fonts; handwritten accents only when meaningful. Avoid microtype for substantive text. Body 16–18px, comfortable line height 1.6–1.8, 60–75 characters per prose line; metadata typically 11–13px; mobile body at least 16px. Fluid display type, roughly 48–92px desktop, 38–54px mobile, checked for wrapping. Consistent baseline and alignment. Use responsive editorial grid, 24–80px edge gutters, section spacing 64–120px. No dashboard grids, pill overload, constant movement, giant empty gaps, nested boxes, fake paper noise or irrelevant scientific symbols. Use whitespace, hairline rules, editorial numbers, intentional cropping, and composition. Retain semantic HTML and existing accessible components.

## 5. Research signature: cellular energy
Replace the current membranePoints generic polygon and independent crista curves. Establish hand-authored normalized Bezier anatomy first: asymmetrical bean-like elongated silhouette, continuous outer membrane, separate continuous inner membrane with several deeply curved invaginations (cristae) connected to it, visible matrix and narrow intermembrane space. No crossing folds, random floating lines, evenly spaced detached squiggles or malformed oval. Keep a recognizable silhouette at 160px and 320px. Use subtle translucent shading, rim light, restrained specular highlights and depth; simple forms are welcome. No static raster illustration as the transition. Draw the fully formed pose and inspect it at rest before animating. Build paths as separate layers/masks; attach folds to the inner membrane, clip effects appropriately, preserve proportions. Treat glow as a visual metaphor for energy, not a literal ATP explosion; avoid misleading biology.

Storyboard (2.5s baseline): 0–0.65 curved luminous strokes and sparse particles travel from surrounding composition; 0.65–1.25 outer/inner membranes form, folds resolve in sequence; 1.25–1.75 a brief recognizable hold while a controlled cyan light travels along the inner membrane, energy builds; 1.75–2.5 the light expands as a soft luminous wave that reveals the real mounted research destination. Aim for a coherent full-view reveal, not a hard-edged donut, a simple fade or a separate card. Some fragments may settle into actual heading rules, but only if it looks intentional. Do not forcibly interpolate an organelle into a long rectangle. Keep the brightest moment short and non-strobing. No full-white flash. Use seeded variation and curved paths, not all particles on a uniform radial ring. Use true viewport geometry and safe travel bounds. Mobile: smaller particles, fewer paths, recognizable shape centered in visible content. Do not stretch scene across the entire scrollable article or place it at a hardcoded y=260 when the viewport differs. Keep navigation/skip available.

## 6. About signature: information becomes identity
Build a distinct scene, not a recolored Research transition. A single mRNA strand enters, upper/lower ribosomal subunits assemble, tRNAs arrive and leave sequentially, the ribosome advances 5'→3' through a short valid codon sequence. Example AUG GCU UUU GGU UAA -> Met-Ala-Phe-Gly, stop releases the chain, no amino acid attached to stop. Show connected beads as a growing chain emerging from the large subunit; use discreet colors and recognizable silhouettes, avoid dense labels. Do not animate mismatched codons/amino acids or present a simplistic diagram as a molecular simulation. About and Research share pacing/palette, not the exact same curtain or mask sequence.

Do not block navigation on animation or network fetches. Play only on deliberate first meaningful navigation per session, not initial deep links or repeated back/forward; optional replay in lab. Skip with button/Escape, reduced motion immediate, no trap or hidden focus. Clean up RAF, observers, styles, masks, events and aria-busy on completion, cancellation, unmount, scroll/resize failures, rapid navigation. If tab hidden or scene offscreen, settle or pause without leaving user stuck. Ensure no accidental first-frame artifact, no leftover blank curtain, no layout shift. Use transform/opacity and bounded particle counts where practical; avoid large full-page blur/repeated expensive per-frame geometry work. Aim for smooth 60fps on typical capable devices; measure honestly, adapt to weaker hardware. Keep motion decorative and aria-hidden, destination readable through assistive technology. Focus management appropriate to navigation, no flashing.
